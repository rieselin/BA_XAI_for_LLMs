Beschreibung\\
The aim of this bachelor thesis is to investigate technical methods of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) for Large Language Models (LLMs), implement selected approaches in a prototype, and empirically evaluate their impact on user trust.\\
To this end, the thesis should:\\
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Review the relevant foundations of LLMs, XAI methods, and trust in AI systems,
    \item Select and technically implement 1–2 XAI approaches for LLMs (e.g., token highlighting, attention visualization, or textual explanations) in a simple demonstrator (e.g., web interface or notebook),
    \item Design and conduct a user study that measures trust and perceived transparency with and without explanations,
    \item Analyze the results and critically discuss them with respect to design recommendations for trustworthy LLM-based applications.
\end{enumerate}
